The long edge goes around the cable. The words run along it.

1 Print the repeat

Several copies of the same text fit down the label. Use a short ID for larger letters.

2 Wrap around the jacket

Peel the label and roll its long edge around the cable. Press the overlapping ends together.

3 Turn it to read it

A repeated copy is easier to find as the cable turns. Keep the seam away from a complete copy.
